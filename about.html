Simple Steps to Order Online

Ordering from HomeMadeGifts is easy and secure. Follow these steps to receive your perfect handmade product or gift hamper right at your doorstep.

1.

Explore & Select

Browse our Home page to discover various categories like Gift Hampers, Marriage Gifts, and Homemade Treats. Click on any item to view full details, images, videos, price, and available discounts.

Remember to log in first so your cart and orders are saved to your account!

2.

Add to Cart

When you find an item you love, use the 'Add to Cart' button. Review your selections on the Cart page, where you can easily remove items (❌) or adjust quantities.

3.

Secure Checkout

Proceed to Checkout. If you are a first-time user, look out for special introductory offers! Provide your contact details and shipping address, including a map location if possible.

4.

Choose Payment & Confirm

Select your preferred method: Cash on Delivery (COD) or Online Payment. Once confirmed, your order details are instantly sent to our admin team via Gmail and WhatsApp for immediate processing.

5.

Delivery & Enjoy!

Our admin, Pragati Babannavar, will personally handle your order. You will receive updates via email or WhatsApp. Sit back, relax, and await your handcrafted package!
